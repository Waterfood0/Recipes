Salad Ceaser

Describtion:

A Caesar salad is a green salad made with romaine lettuce, croutons, and Parmesan cheese, tossed in a rich, tangy dressing

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups crusty bread (baguette or sourdough), torn or cubed
    •
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
    •
  • 1 garlic clove, mince
    •
  • Pinch of kosher salt and black pepper
    •
  • 1 large egg yolk (pasteurized recommended)
    •
  • 1 to 2 garlic cloves, minced or mashed into a paste
    •
  • 2 to 3 oil-packed anchovy fillets, minced or mashed (or 1 tsp anchovy paste)
    •
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
    •
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
    •
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauc
    •
  • ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil (or a mix of neutral oil and olive oil)
    •
  • ¼ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
    •
  • Salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
    •
  • 1 large head (or 2 small heads) of Romaine lettuce, washed, dried, and torn/chopped into bite-sized pieces
    •
  • Extra shaved or grated Parmesan cheese for topping
    •

Preparation:

  1. Make the croutons: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Toss the bread cubes with olive oil, minced garlic, salt, and pepper. Spread them on a baking sheet and bake for 10 to 15 minutes, until golden and crunchy, stirring halfway through.
  2. Make the dressing: In a medium bowl (or the bottom of a large salad bowl), whisk together the egg yolk, minced garlic, anchovy paste/fillets, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, and Worcestershire sauce until smooth.
  3. Emulsify: Slowly stream in the olive oil while whisking constantly (or blend using an immersion blender) until the mixture is thick and creamy.
  4. Finish the dressing: Stir in the grated Parmesan cheese and season with a pinch of salt and black pepper to taste.
  5. Assemble the salad: Add the chopped Romaine lettuce and the crispy croutons directly into the bowl with the dressing. Toss gently until every leaf is evenly coated.
  6. Serve: Top with extra shaved Parmesan cheese and freshly cracked black pepper.
