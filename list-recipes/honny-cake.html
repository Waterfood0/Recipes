Honny Cake

Describtion:

Borscht is a traditional Eastern European sour soup with a characteristic deep red color, originating from Ukraine. In 2022, Ukrainian borscht cooking culture was inscribed on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 lbs beef chuck roast or bone-in beef/pork ribs, cut into chunks
    •
  • 8 cups beef or chicken broth (plus extra water if needed)
    •
  • 2 dried bay leaves
    •
  • 3 medium beets, peeled and shredded or cut into matchsticks
    •
  • 3 medium potatoes, peeled and cubed
    •
  • 2 to 3 carrots, peeled and grated or diced
    •
  • 1/2 head of white or red cabbage, thinly sliced
    •
  • 1 large onion, chopped
    •
  • 2 to 3 cloves garlic, minced
    •
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter
    •
  • 3 to 4 tbsp tomato paste or tomato sauce
    •
  • 1 tsp white vinegar or fresh lemon juice
    •
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
    •
  • Sour cream (smetana) and fresh dill or parsley
    •

Preparation:

  1. Place the beef or ribs in a large pot with 8 cups of water or broth and bay leaves. Bring to a boil, skim off any foam on top, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 45 to 60 minutes until the meat starts to get tender. Remove the meat, shred it, and set it aside
  2. Add the cubed potatoes and shredded beets to the simmering broth. Cook for about 10 to 15 minutes until they begin to soften
  3. In a separate skillet, heat oil or butter over medium heat. Saut the chopped onions and grated carrots until soft and golden. Stir in the tomato paste (or sauce) and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes until darkened slightly
  4. Add the shredded cabbage, the sauted onion-carrot-tomato mixture, and the shredded meat back into the main soup pot. Simmer for another 10 to 15 minutes until the cabbage and all vegetables are tender
  5. Stir in the minced garlic, lemon juice (or vinegar), salt, and black pepper. Turn off the heat, cover the pot, and let the borscht rest for 10 to 20 minutes before serving.
  6. Ladle into bowls and top with a generous dollop of sour cream and fresh chopped dill.
