Honny Cake

Describtion:

Borscht is a traditional Eastern European sour soup with a characteristic deep red color, originating from Ukraine. In 2022, Ukrainian borscht cooking culture was inscribed on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list

Ingredients:

  • 7 tbsp (100g) unsalted butter
    •
  • 4 tbsp (80g) honey
    •
  • ¾ cup (150g) sugar
    •
  • 3 large eggs
    •
  • 1 tsp (5g) baking soda
    •
  • 3½ cups (440g) all-purpose flour
    •
  • 2¼ cups (515g) sour cream
    •
  • 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
    •
  • 1½ cups (190g) powdered sugar
    •
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
    •

Preparation:

  1. Boil water in a medium saucepan and place a heatproof bowl over it to create a double boiler.
  2. Whisk eggs and sugar in the bowl until pale, then stir in the butter and honey until melted and smooth.
  3. Add baking soda to the hot mixture, whisk vigorously as it foams up, and remove from the heat.
  4. Stir in the flour gradually until a soft dough forms, wrap it, and let it rest for 20 to 30 minutes.
  5. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and divide the rested dough into 8 equal pieces.
  6. Roll each piece extremely thin on parchment paper and lightly trace an 8-inch circle template.
  7. Prick the dough with a fork and bake for 7 to 8 minutes until golden amber.
  8. Cut out the circle immediately while warm, setting the circle aside and saving the edge scraps.
  9. Crush all baked scraps into fine crumbs using a food processor or rolling pin once cooled.
  10. Whisk the sour cream in a bowl until smooth and free of lumps.
  11. Whip heavy cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla in a separate bowl to medium-stiff peaks.
  12. Fold the sour cream gently into the whipped cream until thick and combined.
  13. Layer the cake by alternating cake rounds with 4 to 5 tablespoons of frosting.
  14. Coat the top and sides with the remaining frosting, then press the crumbs completely over the exterior.
  15. Chill the cake covered in the refrigerator for 8 to 12 hours before slicing.
